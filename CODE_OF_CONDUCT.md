# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
Code of Conduct. By participating, you agree to uphold this code.

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
